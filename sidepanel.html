Capture the page you're on

  1. Open a page in Chrome: an Amazon or Goodreads book or author page, a Kickstarter project, an Instagram, X, Threads, Facebook or LinkedIn profile, a LinkedIn, Indeed or Glassdoor job post, a Reddit hiring post, Google Maps, or any website.
  2. Press the button. On a list of results, it captures everyone on it.

Automatic search

It opens a small window and goes through the sites you pick, one page at a time at a human pace. Leave that window open (behind others is fine).

        How it works
        
    
          
      
        1. Set up. Sign in with Google, create your leads sheet and add your details. That's all that's needed.
          2. 
      
        2. Find leads. Capture the page you're on, let the automatic search go through book sites, social media and job posts, search Apollo, or type someone in. The tool finds each person's website and email, checks the email works and looks for job posts. Tick the good ones and save them to your sheet.
          3. 
      
        3. Write and send. Each lead gets a short personal email (under 300 characters) written from what was found about them. Edit it if you like and send it from your Gmail. Follow-ups are prepared for you after 4 and 11 days if there's no reply.
          4. 
      
        4. Results. Replies and bounces are picked up every 10 minutes. You get a notification when someone answers.
